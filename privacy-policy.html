Privacy Policy – RaceTurbo

Last updated: September 21, 2026

RaceTurbo is an Android racing game developed by Piotr Tuszynski. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when using the application.

Information Collection

RaceTurbo does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as name, email address, phone number, contacts, messages, photos or other personal files.

Advertising

RaceTurbo uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically collect or process certain information, including:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Device and operating system information
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be used by Google to provide, measure, personalize and improve advertising services, depending on the user's consent and applicable regulations.

More information about Google's privacy practices can be found here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Information about Google's advertising technologies:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

App Permissions

RaceTurbo does not require access to contacts, SMS messages, phone calls, camera, microphone, photos or personal files for normal gameplay.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services such as Google AdMob. These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

RaceTurbo does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party advertising services may process technical information in accordance with their own policies and applicable laws.

Data Security

RaceTurbo does not maintain its own online database containing users' personal information. Third-party service providers are responsible for protecting information processed through their services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, advertising services, or legal requirements change. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

Developer: Piotr Tuszynski

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store page.
